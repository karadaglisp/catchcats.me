The problem

Nobody knows how many stray cats live in Greece.

Official register
323,303
Sector estimate
~3,000,000

That gap is the whole story. You can’t neuter, treat or protect animals nobody has counted - and the only people who know which cat lives on which corner are the neighbours who feed it every evening.

National Companion Animal Registry, June 2025, via CNN Greece and ECOPRESS. The three-million estimate covers cats and dogs and has never been officially verified.
